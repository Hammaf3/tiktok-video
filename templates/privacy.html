4. How We Process YouTube URLs

🔍 YouTube URL Processing:
  1. URL Submission: You provide a YouTube video URL through our interface
  2. Video Download: Our server downloads the video using yt-dlp (a command-line tool)
  3. Format Conversion: The video is converted to TikTok format (9:16 aspect ratio, H.264 codec) using FFmpeg
  4. Temporary Storage: Both original and converted videos are stored temporarily on our server
  5. Delivery: You can download the converted video or upload it directly to TikTok
  6. Automatic Deletion: Video files are automatically deleted after processing or after a maximum retention period

5. TikTok Authorization and Connection

5.1 OAuth Authorization Flow

When you connect your TikTok account:

  • You are redirected to TikTok's official OAuth authorization page
  • You grant permissions for video upload and publishing
  • TikTok provides an access token which we store securely
  • We use this token only to upload videos on your behalf when you request it

5.2 What We Do With TikTok Access

  • ✅ Upload videos to your TikTok account when you explicitly request upload
  • ✅ Store access tokens securely in your session and environment variables
  • ❌ We DO NOT access your TikTok account without your explicit action
  • ❌ We DO NOT automatically post or modify content on TikTok
  • ❌ We DO NOT access your TikTok messages, followers, or other private data
